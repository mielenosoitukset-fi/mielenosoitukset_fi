- Deleting a user in the admin now also handles the account's other records: sign-in data and settings are deleted, ordinary personal records are deleted (kept for admin and other privileged accounts), and abuse evidence and audit records are kept with the person's id, email, username and display name replaced by a stable `poistettu-…` alias. The real identity is stored only encrypted to an offline key. A daily job deletes the kept records and the sealed identity after two years (`ERASURE_RETENTION_DAYS`). See `docs/account-erasure.md`.
