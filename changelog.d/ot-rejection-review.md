- Every rejection and take-down now waits for OT review, as the moderation guide of 18 November 2026 requires. The submitter's email says the rejection is provisional and when it will be reviewed (target two working days, at most five). OT confirms, overturns (the demonstration is published) or asks for more information in Hallinto, and the submitter gets the outcome with its reasons. A confirmed rejection can be reconsidered once by someone other than the original decider within 30 days. If a review runs past five working days, the submitter is told once. Nothing is confirmed automatically.
