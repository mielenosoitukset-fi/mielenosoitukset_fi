- Admin: taking a demonstration off the site now needs a second admin. A normal request keeps the demo public until another admin confirms it ("Vahvista poisto") or declines it; the requester can withdraw it. An urgent take-down ("Kiireellinen") hides the demo at once and another admin confirms it within 48 hours; the case shows when confirmation is late. Nobody can confirm their own request. Settings: `TAKE_DOWN_SECOND_ADMIN` (false = one admin is enough) and `TAKE_DOWN_CONFIRM_HOURS`.
