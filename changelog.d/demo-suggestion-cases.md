- Demonstration change suggestions can now appear in the admin case list as "Muutosehdotus" cases, so they no longer wait unnoticed on the separate suggestions page. The case links straight to the existing review page and closes itself once the suggestion is applied, rejected or closed. City admins see only suggestions for their own cities. Off until `DEMO_SUGGESTION_CASES_ENABLED` is set; when switched on, suggestions that are still open also get a case.
- New cases can now send a notice to the site inbox (the bell) of every admin who may view the case, with a direct link. The notice disappears once the case is closed or the admin loses access. Off until `CASE_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED` is set.
- New cases can also be e-mailed. Admins with case rights everywhere always get the mail; admins who handle cases only for their own cities can turn it on or off on the case list. Each case page lists which admins were notified and which were mailed. Off until `CASE_NOTIFICATION_EMAILS_ENABLED` is set as well; the support mailbox keeps its current mails.
